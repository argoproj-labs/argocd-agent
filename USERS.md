## How to Add Your Organization

We love hearing about argocd-agent adoption! To add your organization:

1. **Fork this repository**
2. **Edit this file** and add your organization under the appropriate section
   - Use alphabetical order within each section
   - Format: `- **[Organization Name](https://example.com)** - Brief description of your use case (optional)`
   - Example: `- **Acme Corp** - Managing 100+ edge clusters across retail locations`
3. **Submit a pull request** with the title "Add [Organization] to USERS.md"

### Guidelines

- **Be respectful**: This list showcases community adoption
- **Be accurate**: Only add organizations you represent or have permission from
- **Be brief**: Keep descriptions concise (1-2 sentences maximum)
- **Provide value**: Sharing your use case helps others learn from your experience

### Need Help?

If you have questions about adding your organization or want to share a more detailed story:

Publicly sharing your adoption helps:
- **Demonstrate maturity**: Real-world usage signals project stability
- **Build community**: Connect with other users facing similar challenges
- **Influence direction**: Your use cases help shape future development
- **Give back**: Recognition for the open-source community that built this
